# Dub Sub Hub logo concepts — v7

Three new layouts extend the [v6 orange and white direction](../v6/README.md). Each keeps the dialogue relay on the left, an orange gradient pill, balanced word widths, and a flat white Hub tile.

| Concept | Full logo | Icon | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relay Capsule | [SVG](01-relay-capsule.svg) | [SVG](01-relay-capsule-icon.svg) | A wide capsule with overlapping audio and caption bubbles; all words span 250 units with 72-unit gaps |
| Caption Bridge | [SVG](02-caption-bridge.svg) | [SVG](02-caption-bridge-icon.svg) | A two-row pill with a bridge arrow; Dub and Sub sit above a broad white Hub tile and every word spans 235 units |
| Dialogue Tower | [SVG](03-dialogue-tower.svg) | [SVG](03-dialogue-tower-icon.svg) | A tall pill with stacked dialogue bubbles; 340-unit words share one left edge and 280-unit baseline steps |

[Compare all three concepts](04-comparison.svg)

Matching PNG previews are supplied beside each SVG, including [the comparison sheet](04-comparison.png).

Palette: gradient `#FF8744` → `#FF5C35` → `#EF4530`, relay orange `#D94B27`, Hub orange `#EE4C2C`, and white `#FFFFFF`. Hub tiles stay flat with no shadows. Each standalone icon carries the relay from its full logo on an orange gradient square.

All files use editable vector geometry with descriptive IDs and classes. The wordmarks remain live SVG text; `textLength` controls their widths, while installed font fallbacks can change the letter shapes and optical spacing. Full logos and icons have transparent margins; the comparison sheet provides a light review background.
