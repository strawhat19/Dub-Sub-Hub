# Dub Sub Hub

Dub Sub Hub is a concept for making video accessible in the language you want to hear or read. The goal is to work with video players across services such as YouTube, Netflix, HBO Max, and others, subject to each platform's technical and content restrictions.

## Product vision

- **Translate subtitles:** Turn existing captions into subtitles in a viewer's chosen language while preserving their timing and meaning.
- **Generate subtitles:** Transcribe speech and create timed subtitles when a video has no captions, then translate them if requested.
- **Dub audio:** Replace spoken dialogue with expressive AI voice acting in a chosen language. The intended audio workflow separates voices from the source and keeps music, effects, and ambience in place so the new dialogue does not interrupt the rest of the soundtrack.

The viewer should be able to choose a language and switch between original audio, translated subtitles, generated subtitles, and dubbed audio from the player experience.

## Status

`v1/` contains early branding and product direction. The capabilities above describe the intended app, not an implemented feature set.
